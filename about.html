About

Tech Workbench is a set of free, in-browser tools for students and engineers: a number base converter, chmod calculator, subnet calculator, truth-table generator, K-map simplifier, flip-flop simulator and CPU scheduling simulator. Everything runs in your browser, and nothing you type is sent to a server.

The site is run by Mibek Pandey. Corrections and suggestions are welcome through the contact page.
