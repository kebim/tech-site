## Editorial Policy

Learn & Tech aims to publish original, useful, and clearly organized educational content. Roadmaps, study notes, tool guides, and news summaries should explain what a learner can do next, why a resource matters, what assumptions apply, and where a reader can verify important details.

For technical claims that may change, the preferred source is official documentation, a standards body, a vendor’s primary announcement, or another reputable primary source. Technology news entries should state a publication date, name the source, link to the original announcement, summarize it in original language, and distinguish reporting from commentary.

When a correction is needed, readers may contact the publisher using the contact information above. Material corrections will be made to the relevant page when practical.

Advertising, affiliate links, sponsorships, or paid relationships—if they are added in the future—will be clearly distinguished from editorial content. Advertising will not be presented as a navigation item, download button, required learning step, or editorial recommendation.
